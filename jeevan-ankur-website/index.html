Meet our founder

The heart and dedication behind Jeevan Ankur Seva Foundation and the home where our senior citizens find care, dignity and a family to come home to.

Under our founder's devoted care, our old age home in Kurla, Mumbai provides a loving, protected environment where every elderly resident receives proper healthcare, nutritious meals, companionship, and genuine familial respect.
