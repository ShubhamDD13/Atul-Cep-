Our Services

Everything a senior citizen needs to live safely and happily under one roof.

Residential care

A safe, comfortable place to live for senior citizens who are alone or need support.

Food and accommodation

Regular, nutritious meals and a clean place to sleep and rest.

Healthcare support

Medical assistance and help getting the care each resident needs.

Personal care and assistance

Daily help with the things that become hard with age.

Recreational activities

Games, music and time together to keep days lively.

Emotional and social support

Someone to talk to, listen to and spend time with.

Be part of their family

Donate, visit, or simply spend an afternoon with our residents.
